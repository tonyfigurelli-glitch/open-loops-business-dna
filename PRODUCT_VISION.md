## Purpose

Provide a concise product-vision entry point for Open Loops, linking the long-term company vision to the Version 1 product direction.

## Product Vision Summary

Open Loops is a personal understanding platform designed to help people capture thoughts, connect ideas, and gradually discover patterns within their own thinking.

The long-term vision is a Universal Emotional Translator. The Version 1 entry point is a beautiful, calm, visual way to capture and connect thoughts through Open Loops and Lumi.

## Version 1 Product Direction

- Capture thoughts
- Create Open Loops
- Talk to Lumi
- Organize thinking through bubbles
- Preserve continuity through persistent storage
- Avoid exposing deeper long-term capabilities too early
